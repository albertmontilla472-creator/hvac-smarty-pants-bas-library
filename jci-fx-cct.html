Johnson Controls FX / CCT

Keep Facility Explorer supervisory work separate from CCT controller applications. Some CCT tutorials demonstrate Metasys hardware; each resource identifies the demonstrated family and release context.

JCI documentation identifies CCT as a configuration, simulation, and commissioning tool for the covered FX controller family. The three-day recordings are Advanced CCT training, aimed at experienced personnel. Do not assume their release or controller support matches your future installation.
