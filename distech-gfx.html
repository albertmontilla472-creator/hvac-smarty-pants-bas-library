Distech EC-gfxProgram

EC-gfxProgram is the controller programming subject here. EC-Net/Niagara supervisory work, ECLYPSE tools and ENVYSION graphics remain labeled as related but separate workflows.

The official Productivity Enhancing Tools playlist has 30 entries, including English/French duplicates and product demos. It is a feature collection, not a full beginner programming course. Many selected videos show older interfaces; the emulator video explicitly covers v6.0.
