# The Controls Exchange — Free BAS Learning

Curated by **Smarty.ctrl**. Follow [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/smarty.ctrl/) or connect on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/albert-montilla-593356318/). These are direct profile links; no social embeds or tracking scripts load here.

A free, independent directory of BAS videos, documentation and learning paths, covering Niagara, Honeywell Optimizer/Spyder, Johnson Controls Facility Explorer/CCT and Distech EC-gfxProgram.

The Controls Exchange uses an original red, charcoal and white learning workbench with flat toolbars, quiet selection and clear reading space. Home belongs under Station above Config. Branch +/− controls only expand/collapse; section labels navigate, and Ctrl/Cmd-click opens independent workspaces. Drag the sidebar edge to widen both panes or the horizontal divider to adjust tree/palette height. Subfolders and advanced filters expand on demand. The Learning palette owns recommended creators, news, release watch, downloads and personal study tools. Creator links, study progress and review history appear at the bottom of Home.

Training covers Niagara AX/N4/N5, Honeywell Optimizer/IRM and Classic Spyder, JCI FX/CCT and Distech EC-gfxProgram. Resource properties show the original publisher, controller generation, recording/version context and licensed-practice notes. Optional bookmarks, study marks and notes stay in the browser; export a backup to move them. Original interface branding and artwork do not imply affiliation with the software vendors.

The directory credits and links to original publishers. Third-party videos, manuals and installers are not rehosted. Only free public learning material is listed. Paid training, enrollment catalogs and partner-only references are excluded. Free lessons may demonstrate tools that require a separate practice license. The project has no advertising, affiliate links or paid placements; its purpose is learning, creator credit and community benefit. Original study worksheets are included for personal learning.
