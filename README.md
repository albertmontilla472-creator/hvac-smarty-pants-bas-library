# HVAC Smarty Pants — Free BAS Learning

A free, independent directory of BAS videos, documentation and learning paths, covering Niagara, Honeywell Optimizer/Spyder, Johnson Controls Facility Explorer/CCT and Distech EC-gfxProgram.

The HVAC Smarty Pants learning workbench uses warm ivory chrome, olive-gray frames, white content panes and narrow light-blue navigation headers. Its Nav tree has sibling Platform and Station branches, with Config, Services, Drivers, Files and History folders organizing the free learning resources. Niagara training is divided into AX, N4 and N5, with separate videos and documentation. N5 is labeled as readiness/preview material until general release is verified. Counts describe the actual sections or matching items in each folder. Folder navigation and lesson property sheets share the large main pane. Choose a lesson to see its original creator, tool and controller generation, recording context and access notes. Optional bookmarks, study marks and notes stay in the visitor’s browser; they are not uploaded. Export a study backup to move them between devices.

The directory credits and links to original publishers. Third-party videos, manuals and installers are not rehosted. Only free public learning material is listed. Paid training, enrollment catalogs and partner-only references are excluded. Free lessons may demonstrate tools that require a separate practice license. The project has no advertising, affiliate links or paid placements; its purpose is learning, creator credit and community benefit. Original study worksheets are included for personal learning.
