    BEGINNERS ONLY · BAS FOUNDATIONS

    Beginner BAS study sequence

    New to building automation? Start with these six foundation blocks from Smart Buildings Academy, then take the optional Niagara 4 orientation from Broudy Precision if you are new to that platform. No Optimizer, IRM or other vendor controller programming is required for this path. Experienced users can browse the Nav tree or use the top name search to find their training.

    After the basics, choose the vendor or tool you work with from the Nav tree. Programming paths contains the more involved tool-specific routes and existing practical projects.

    What the labels mean

    Foundation / Next / Advanced / Reference: our study suggestions, not vendor certification prerequisites.

    Free public resource: a publicly accessible video, guide or collection is listed. No payment, course enrollment or partner login is required for the listed learning material. Playback was not tested end to end.

    Recording context: older demos and explicit versions remain useful references; check your actual release before following procedures.

    Support the people who teach

    Watch on the original channel, explore the creator’s other free lessons, and thank them if their work helps you. The credit and audience belong with the people who made the material.
